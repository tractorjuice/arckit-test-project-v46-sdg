## Executive Summary

**Purpose**: The Citizen Participation Platform delivers a cross-government digital platform for public consultations and citizen engagement, replacing fragmented departmental approaches with a standardised, accessible, and transparent system that demonstrably connects citizen input to policy outcomes.

**Problem Statement**: Government consultations are perceived as tokenistic (18% public trust), fragmented across 20+ tools, expensive (average GBP 80K per consultation), slow (9-month average cycle), and fail to reach diverse communities. This undermines democratic legitimacy and wastes policy development resources.

**Proposed Solution**: A standardised digital platform with consultation builder, accessible response interface, NLP-based analysis, mandatory outcome reporting, and deliberative engagement features (discussion forums, participatory budgeting).

**Strategic Fit**: Delivers the UK's OGP National Action Plan citizen engagement commitments, supports SDG 16.7 (responsive, inclusive, participatory decision-making), aligns with the Levelling Up agenda, and complies with the Nolan Principles of Public Life.

**Investment Required**: GBP 8M over 3 years

- Capital: GBP 6M
- Operational (3 years): GBP 2M

**Expected Benefits**: GBP 18M over 5 years

- Consultation cost savings across government: GBP 10M (500 consultations x GBP 40K average saving)
- Reduced FOI/correspondence costs through proactive engagement: GBP 3M
- Policy quality improvement (fewer costly policy failures from inadequate consultation): GBP 3M
- Local authority efficiency (shared platform): GBP 2M

**Return on Investment**:

- NPV: GBP 8M (discounted at 3.5%)
- Payback Period: 20 months
- ROI: 125%

**Recommended Option**: Option 3: Build standardised cross-government platform with deliberative features

**Key Risks**:

1. Departmental adoption — departments may resist standardisation
2. Low citizen participation despite platform improvements
3. NLP analysis quality for thematic summarisation of open-text responses
4. Discussion forum moderation at scale

**Go/No-Go Recommendation**: **PROCEED**

**Rationale**: The GBP 8M investment replaces GBP 10M+ annual cross-government consultation spend with a standardised, more effective approach. The real value extends beyond cost savings — improved policy quality through genuine citizen engagement avoids costly policy failures and rebuilds democratic trust. The Levelling Up agenda, OGP commitments, and SDG 16.7 obligations provide strong policy drivers. The platform also supports local government, creating a shared national infrastructure for democratic engagement.

# PART A: STRATEGIC CASE

## A1. Strategic Context

### A1.1 Problem Statement

**Current Situation**: Government departments run 500+ formal consultations per year using a patchwork of tools — some use commercial platforms (Citizen Space, SmartSurvey), others use PDF documents with email responses, and several have built bespoke solutions. There is no cross-government standard, no central reporting, and no consistency in how outcomes are published. Response analysis is manual for most departments.

**Specific Pain Points**:

| Stakeholder | Pain Point | Impact |
|-------------|------------|--------|
| DLUHC Minister | Only 18% public trust in consultations | Democratic legitimacy deficit |
| Cabinet Office | 500+ consultations with no standard process | Inconsistent quality, no cross-govt reporting |
| Citizens | Consultations feel tokenistic, no feedback on impact | Participation fatigue, disengagement |
| Departmental teams | GBP 80K average cost, 9-month cycle | Expensive, slow policy development |

**Consequences of Inaction**:

# PART B: ECONOMIC CASE

## B1. Options

### Option 1: Do Nothing

**Costs**: GBP 40M over 5 years (current departmental spending on consultation tools and processes)
**Benefits**: GBP 0
**Assessment**: Trust continues to decline. OGP commitments undelivered. Unacceptable.

### Option 2: Procure Single Commercial Platform (e.g., Citizen Space)

**Description**: Standardise all government consultations on a single commercial platform.
**Costs**: GBP 2M capital + GBP 8M licensing over 5 years = GBP 10M
**Benefits**: GBP 12M (efficiency savings but limited customisation)
**NPV**: GBP 2M
**Assessment**: Delivers standardisation but limited flexibility for deliberative features. Vendor lock-in. No NLP analysis. No participatory budgeting. Does not address the trust deficit.

### Option 3: Build Cross-Government Platform with Deliberative Features (RECOMMENDED)

**Description**: Build a government-owned platform with consultation management, NLP analysis, outcome reporting enforcement, discussion forums, and participatory budgeting.
**Costs**: GBP 6M capital + GBP 2M operational (3 years) + GBP 2M (years 4-5) = GBP 10M
**Benefits**: GBP 18M over 5 years
**NPV**: GBP 8M
**BCR**: 1.8
**Assessment**: Best value. Addresses both efficiency and democratic legitimacy. Government ownership avoids lock-in. Deliberative features differentiate from commercial alternatives.

### Option 4: Federated Approach — Guidance Only

**Description**: Publish improved guidance and standards for consultations but leave departments to choose their own tools.
**Costs**: GBP 1M (guidance development)
**Benefits**: GBP 3M (minor improvements)
**NPV**: GBP 2M
**Assessment**: Low cost but does not address the fundamental problem. No standardisation, no cross-government reporting, no deliberative infrastructure.
