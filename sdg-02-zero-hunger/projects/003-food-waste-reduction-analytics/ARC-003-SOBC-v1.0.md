## Document Purpose

This SOBC sets out the strategic justification for a Food Waste Reduction Analytics platform, following HM Treasury Green Book methodology and the Five Case Model.

---

## Executive Summary

**Purpose**: The Food Waste Reduction Analytics platform will provide DEFRA and WRAP with a comprehensive data system for measuring, tracking, and reducing food waste across the UK supply chain, supporting the government's commitment to halve food waste by 2030.

**Problem Statement**: The UK generates approximately 9.5 million tonnes of food waste annually (£19B value), but current measurement has +/-25% uncertainty and 12-18 month reporting lag. Without improved data, the government cannot credibly report SDG 12.3 progress, target interventions effectively, or hold the supply chain to account.

**Proposed Solution**: Build a data analytics platform integrating waste data from industry participants (via Courtauld Commitment), local authorities (WasteDataFlow), and the Environment Agency, providing near-real-time waste measurement, anonymised benchmarking, and waste hierarchy tracking.

**Strategic Fit**: Directly supports the Resources and Waste Strategy 2018 (food waste reduction pillar), SDG 12.3 commitments, Environment Act 2021 waste provisions, and the Courtauld Commitment 2030.

**Investment Required**: £6.5M over 3 years

- Capital: £4.1M
- Operational (3 years): £2.4M

**Expected Benefits**: £22.0M over 5 years

- Industry waste reduction enabled by benchmarking insights: £15.0M
- WRAP operational efficiency (automated vs manual reporting): £3.0M
- Carbon and environmental value of waste diversion: £4.0M

**Return on Investment**:

- NPV: £12.4M (discounted at 3.5%)
- Payback Period: 22 months
- ROI: 238%

**Recommended Option**: Option 2: Integrated Analytics Platform with Industry Benchmarking

**Key Risks**:

1. Industry may not adopt voluntary data reporting at sufficient scale
2. Methodological inconsistencies across supply chain stages may limit data comparability
3. Integration with WRAP legacy systems may be more complex than estimated

**Go/No-Go Recommendation**: **PROCEED**

**Rationale**: Strong economic case driven by the environmental and commercial value of food waste reduction. The platform enables evidence-based policy and provides industry with actionable insights. Partnership with WRAP leverages existing industry relationships.

# PART A: STRATEGIC CASE

## A1. Strategic Context

### A1.1 Problem Statement

**Current Situation**: WRAP produces biennial estimates of UK food waste using a combination of industry reporting (annual Courtauld spreadsheets), waste composition analyses, and statistical modelling. The latest published figures are from 2022 data, published in 2024. This 12-18 month lag means policy interventions are based on outdated information.

**Consequences of Inaction**:

- UK cannot credibly report SDG 12.3 progress at 2028 UN High-Level Political Forum
- £19B/year in wasted food continues without targeted intervention
- 9.5Mt food waste generates approximately 25Mt CO2e greenhouse gas emissions annually
- Courtauld Commitment renewal at risk if WRAP cannot demonstrate value through data
- UK falls behind EU (mandatory food waste reporting from 2027 under revised Waste Framework Directive)
