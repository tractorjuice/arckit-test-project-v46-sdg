## Document Purpose

This Strategic Outline Business Case sets out the rationale, options, and indicative costs for building the Disability Confident Employer Portal — a DWP-operated platform to digitise the Disability Confident employer accreditation scheme, introduce evidence-based assessment, integrate with Access to Work, and track the disability employment gap at employer level. It follows the HM Treasury Green Book five-case model.

---

## Executive Summary

**Purpose**: Transform the Disability Confident scheme from a paper-based self-assessment process into a digital, evidence-based accreditation platform that measurably contributes to closing the disability employment gap (currently 28 percentage points).

**Problem Statement**: The Disability Confident scheme has 19,000+ employer members but the disability employment gap remains stubbornly wide at 28 percentage points (52% vs 80% employment rate). The Work and Pensions Committee has criticised the scheme as "too easy to pass" through self-assessment. The current paper/email process takes 8 weeks, collects no outcome data, and cannot demonstrate whether participation changes employer behaviour.

**Proposed Solution**: Build a digital employer portal with streamlined accreditation workflows, evidence-based assessment for Levels 2-3, disabled employee feedback, Access to Work referral integration, workforce disability data collection, and public employer profiles for jobseekers.

**Strategic Fit**: Directly supports the National Disability Strategy employment commitments, Equality Act 2010 reasonable adjustments duty, and the government's commitment to halve the disability employment gap.

**Investment Required**: GBP 7.2M over 3 years

- Capital: GBP 3.8M
- Operational (3 years): GBP 3.4M

**Expected Benefits**: GBP 14.6M over 3 years

- Efficiency: GBP 3.2M (process automation, reduced manual handling)
- Employment impact: GBP 8.5M (disability employment gap reduction through improved employer practices)
- Employer value: GBP 1.5M (reduced recruitment costs, improved retention)
- Welfare savings: GBP 1.4M (reduced disability benefit claims through employment)

**Return on Investment**:

- NPV: GBP 6.2M (discounted at 3.5%)
- Payback Period: 18 months
- ROI: 103%

**Recommended Option**: Option 2: Balanced Approach — Digital portal with evidence-based assessment and Access to Work integration

**Key Risks**:

1. Mass employer dropout when evidence requirements are introduced (political sensitivity)
2. Disability charities reject reformed scheme as still insufficient
3. Employers gaming evidence submissions without genuine practice change

**Go/No-Go Recommendation**: **PROCEED**

**Rationale**: The disability employment gap costs the UK economy an estimated GBP 100 billion annually in lost productivity and welfare spending. The Disability Confident scheme is the government's primary employer engagement tool but lacks credibility, outcome measurement, and digital infrastructure. A GBP 7.2M investment to transform the scheme delivers 103% ROI while addressing parliamentary criticism and disability sector concerns.

**Next Steps if Approved**:

1. Secure DWP funding approval: Q2 2026

# PART A: STRATEGIC CASE

## A1. Strategic Context

### A1.1 Problem Statement

**Current Situation**:
The Disability Confident scheme was launched in 2016 to replace the Two Ticks scheme. It has three levels: Level 1 (Committed), Level 2 (Employer), Level 3 (Leader). Over 19,000 employers have signed up, but the scheme relies entirely on self-assessment, collects no outcome data, and operates through PDF forms and email with 6-8 week processing times.

**Specific Pain Points** (from Stakeholder Analysis ARC-004-STKE-v1.0):

| Stakeholder | Driver ID | Pain Point | Impact | Intensity |
|-------------|-----------|------------|--------|-----------|
| DWP Minister | SD-1 | Cannot demonstrate scheme reduces employment gap | Scheme credibility questioned by PAC | CRITICAL |
| Scope | SD-2 | Self-assessment allows badging without behaviour change | Disabled jobseekers misled by DC badge | CRITICAL |
| CBI/FSB | SD-3 | Paper process is slow, duplicative, burdensome | Employer frustration, avoidable admin | HIGH |
| Disabled jobseekers | SD-5 | DC badge is unreliable signal of genuine inclusion | Wasted applications to non-inclusive employers | HIGH |

**Consequences of Inaction**:

- Disability employment gap remains at 28 percentage points — costing GBP 100 billion annually in lost economic output
- Work and Pensions Committee continues to criticise scheme as ineffective
- Disability charities withdraw endorsement, undermining scheme legitimacy
- Employers disengage from government disability employment initiatives
- DWP unable to demonstrate value for money from scheme administration costs

### A1.2 Strategic Drivers

**Strategic Alignment**:

- **National Disability Strategy**: Commitment to halve the disability employment gap
- **Equality Act 2010**: Reasonable adjustments duty for employers
- **Levelling Up White Paper**: Mission 1 (pay, employment, productivity) — disability employment is a component
- **UK Digital Strategy**: Digital-by-default government services
- **Architecture Principles**: Principles 1 (Inclusive Design), 4 (Multi-Channel Access Parity), 20 (Social Model of Disability)

### A1.3 Scope

**In Scope**:

- Digital accreditation workflow (all three levels)
- Evidence-based assessment (Levels 2-3)
- Disabled employee feedback mechanism
- Employer public profiles
- Access to Work referral integration
- Workforce disability data collection
- Public employer search for jobseekers
- DWP scheme analytics dashboard

**Out of Scope**:

- Disability Confident policy design (DWP policy team)
- Access to Work grant processing
- Job matching or vacancy management
- BDF Disability Smart certification

### A1.4 Why Now?

**Urgency Factors**:

- Work and Pensions Committee 2024 report called for "fundamental reform" of the scheme
- Disability employment gap widening post-pandemic for some impairment groups
- 19,000+ employers due for renewal in the next 24 months — opportunity to transition to new system
- National Disability Strategy Action Plan requires measurable progress by 2027

**Opportunity Cost of Delay**:

- GBP 200K per month in DWP manual processing costs for paper-based applications
- Continued reputational damage from parliamentary and charity criticism
- 19,000 employer renewals processed through unreformed paper system
- Each year of delay extends the disability employment gap by an estimated 0.2 percentage points
