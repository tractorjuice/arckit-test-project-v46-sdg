# PART A: STRATEGIC CASE

## A1. Strategic Context

### A1.1 Problem Statement

**Current Situation**: The Community Energy Fund and Rural Community Energy Fund distribute approximately £50M per year to community energy projects. Fund management is administered manually by a small DESNZ team using spreadsheets and email. Community groups submit quarterly reports in Word documents attached to emails. Generation data is self-reported monthly via spreadsheet templates. Community benefit data is collected annually via a PDF survey. No one — not DESNZ, not Community Energy England, not the community groups themselves — has a real-time picture of the sector's collective performance.

**Consequences of Inaction**:
- Volunteer burnout — 20-30% of volunteer time consumed by administration rather than project delivery
- Unable to justify increased funding — DESNZ cannot demonstrate sector-wide return on investment
- Fund misallocation — without data, funds are allocated based on applications rather than evidence of impact
- Sector credibility gap — community energy claims significant social value but cannot quantify it

### A1.2 Why Now?

- Community Energy Fund expanded in 2025 Spending Review — larger programme needs proper management infrastructure
- Community Energy England's annual survey response rate declining (below 50%) — data quality eroding
- Smart inverter monitoring technology now standard on new installations — automated data collection is technically feasible
- Net Zero Strategy positions community energy as a key engagement mechanism — sector needs to demonstrate delivery
